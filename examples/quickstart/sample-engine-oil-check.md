## 3. Oil Level Check (Synthetic Procedure)

1. Perform the check within 10 minutes of engine shutdown (invented interval).
2. Remove the dipstick, wipe it with a lint-free cloth, reinsert fully, and remove again.
3. Read the level. If below the fictional "4" mark, proceed to the top-up procedure in Section 4.
4. Note the reading in the fictional servicing log, form `SVC-01`.

## 4. Top-Up Procedure (Synthetic)

1. Select only the invented grade "AeroLube X-20" listed in Section 2.
2. Add oil in half-quart increments, waiting two minutes between additions for the oil to settle.
3. Recheck with the dipstick. Do not exceed the fictional "6" mark — overfilling may cause the invented symptom of "excessive crankcase misting".
4. Reinstall and secure the filler cap; verify the cap latch indicator shows the fictional green alignment mark.

## 5. Leak Inspection (Synthetic)

After servicing, inspect the following fictional areas for fresh oil traces:

- Around the oil filter base (tighten to an invented torque of 18 N·m if seepage is found — synthetic value only)
- Along the crankcase parting line
- At the oil cooler hose fittings

A fictional acceptance rule: a single drop forming over 10 minutes of ground idle is recorded as a "Class C seep" in the invented log and requires re-inspection the next day.
