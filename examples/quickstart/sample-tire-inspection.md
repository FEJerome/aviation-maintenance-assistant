# Landing Gear Tire Inspection (Synthetic Sample)

> This document is a fully synthetic sample written for format demonstration.
> All pressures, wear limits, and replacement criteria below are invented. Do not apply them to any real aircraft.

## 1. Purpose

This sample describes a fictional tire inspection routine for the made-up nose and main landing gear of the synthetic "SKY-100" training aircraft. It demonstrates how short, table-driven maintenance sections appear in documents the toolkit is designed to handle.

## 2. Synthetic Tire Data

| Position | Invented size | Invented cold inflation pressure |
|----------|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Nose gear | 5.00-5 (fictional) | 45 ± 3 psi (invented) |
| Main gear (each) | 6.00-6 (fictional) | 50 ± 3 psi (invented) |

## 3. Visual Inspection (Synthetic Procedure)

1. Clean the tire surface with water and a soft brush; allow it to dry.
2. Examine the tread for cuts, embedded foreign objects, and uneven wear.
3. Examine the sidewalls for bulges, cracks, and signs of the fictional condition "ozone crazing".
4. Check the wheel rim for corrosion around the bead seat area.

## 4. Invented Wear and Damage Limits

| Finding | Invented serviceable limit | Invented replacement criterion |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Tread depth | Minimum 1.5 mm at any point (fictional) | Below 1.5 mm, or fabric layer visible |
| Single cut | Shorter than 12 mm and not deeper than 2 mm (fictional) | Exceeds either dimension, or fabric exposed |
| Sidewall crack | None permitted on the fictional "bead-to-shoulder zone" | Any crack in that zone |
| Flat spot | None permitted (fictional) | Any visible flat spot |

## 5. Pressure Check (Synthetic)

1. Measure pressure with a calibrated gauge, cold (aircraft not moved for at least 2 hours — invented interval).
2. Compare with the invented values in Section 2.
3. A fictional rule: if a tire loses more than 5% of its pressure within 24 hours, mark it with the invented code `T-LEAK` and remove it from service for investigation.

## 6. Replacement Notes (Synthetic)

- Replace tires in pairs on the same axle (fictional policy) to avoid uneven loading.
- After installation, torque the axle nut to an invented 40 N·m and fit a new cotter pin.
- Record the change in the fictional component log, form `COMP-07`.

## 7. Safety Notes (Synthetic)

- Deflate a tire completely before removing any wheel hardware.
- Never stand in front of or behind a wheel while inflating; use a clip-on chuck and stand to the side.
- This sample contains only invented values. Real tire limits come from the official aircraft and tire manufacturer documentation.
