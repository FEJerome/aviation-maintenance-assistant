3. **Wing leading edges** — check for dents; fictional acceptance: no dent deeper than 3 mm within the outer third of the span.
4. **Control surfaces** — verify full and free movement; check hinges for the fictional "no visible play" criterion.
5. **Antennas and static ports** — confirm they are unobstructed.
